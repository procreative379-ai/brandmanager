Last updated: 1 May 2026

This is an English translation provided for convenience. If it differs from the Russian original, the Russian version applies.

This privacy policy describes how the website brandmanager.su collects, uses and protects users' personal data. By using the website, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Personal data operator

The personal data operator is an individual providing brand management and brand strategy services:

  • Website: brandmanager.su
  • E-mail: i@creativedm.ru
  • Phone: +7 (993) 367-31-78

2. What data we collect

The following categories of data may be collected when you use the website:

  • Data from the contact form: name, phone number, email address, company name, message text
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, time and duration of the visit
  • Cookie data: session identifiers, user preferences, analytics data

3. Purposes of processing personal data

The data collected is used only for the following purposes:

  • Responding to users' requests and enquiries
  • Contacting potential clients about working together
  • Analysing website traffic statistics to improve the website
  • Detecting and fixing technical problems

4. Legal basis for processing

Personal data is processed on the basis of:

  • The consent of the data subject (Article 6 of Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ)
  • Performance of a contract or taking steps to enter into a contract
  • The operator's legitimate interests in analysing how the website works

5. Use of cookies

The website uses cookies, small text files stored in your browser. They are used for:

  • Technical cookies: make the website work correctly (sessions, authorisation)
  • Analytics cookies: Yandex Metrica (counter 72239779) collects statistics on traffic and user behaviour, including Webvisor session recording

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Some website features may then not work correctly. By continuing to use the website, you consent to the use of cookies.

6. Yandex Metrica

The website uses a Yandex Metrica counter (ID: 72239779). The service collects anonymised statistics about visitors, including page views, traffic sources and on-site behaviour. The data is sent to Yandex servers and processed in accordance with the Yandex Privacy Policy. To opt out of data collection, follow the Yandex instructions.

7. Disclosure of data to third parties

Users' personal data is not disclosed to third parties, except:

  • In cases provided for by the current legislation of the Russian Federation
  • To analytics services (Yandex Metrica), and only anonymised statistical data

The data is not sold, not shared for advertising purposes and not used to build user profiles.

8. Data storage and protection

Personal data is stored only for as long as needed to achieve the purposes for which it was collected. Data from contact forms is stored until the request has been dealt with, and for no more than 3 years. Technical and organisational measures are taken to protect the data from unauthorised access.

9. Rights of data subjects

Under Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data", you have the right to:

  • Receive information about the processing of your personal data
  • Request that your data be corrected, blocked or destroyed
  • Withdraw your consent to the processing of personal data
  • Appeal the operator's actions to Roskomnadzor

To exercise these rights, write to: i@creativedm.ru

10. Changes to the policy

The operator reserves the right to make changes to this policy. The current version is always available on this page. The date of the last update is given at the top of the document.
