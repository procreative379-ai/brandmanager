01 / The task

Create a water brand from scratch and get it into national retail chains

Context

Svam Group is a producer and distributor of soft drinks. In 2020 the company decided to launch its own drinking water brand. The market was growing, and private label margins were higher than those of imported brands. The task: create a brand from scratch and get listed with the largest national retail chains within 12 months.

Difficulty

Drinking water is one of the most competitive FMCG markets. The shelf already holds Bon Aqua, Aqua Minerale, Shishkin Les and dozens of regional brands. Without clear positioning and strong support, a new brand simply gets lost. We needed a strategy that would give the buyer a reason to take our product.

04 / How we got listed

From the first meeting to the shelf in 6 weeks

01
We came with numbers

To the first meeting with the buyer we brought a Nielsen analysis of the water category, a 12-month promotion plan with a specific budget, and product cards ready to load into the chain's system

02
We started negotiating before the goods arrived

First contact with the X5 and Lenta buyers was 3 months before delivery. By the time the goods reached the warehouse, the chains were ready to put them on the shelf

03
The marketing budget is part of the negotiation

Retail chains earn on suppliers' promotion budgets as well as on margin. We came with specific figures: promotions, shelf placement, tastings, all with amounts attached

04
Nielsen analysis as an argument

The buyer sees category data every month, but when a supplier comes with the same data and its own analysis, the conversation changes. We showed the segment's growth and an unfilled niche
