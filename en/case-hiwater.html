04 / Brand platform

The brand as a system of meaning

Based on the analysis of the audience, competitors and trends, we developed a full brand platform covering every element from the mission to the archetype and RTB.

🎯 Brand Purpose Why the brand exists in the world: its deeper mission
👁 Brand Vision The world the brand wants to see in the future
💬 Brand Mission What the brand does to reach that vision
💎 Brand Values The principles that shape how the brand behaves
⚡ Brand Essence One central idea that runs through everything
🎭 Brand archetype The role the brand plays in the customer's life, the basis of the emotional connection
✅ RTB (Reasons to Believe) The evidence behind the brand promise
🗣 Tone of Voice How the brand talks to its audience in different channels

Choosing the archetype

To define the archetype we used the brand wheel: 12 universal roles a brand can play in the customer's life. The archetype we chose set HiWater's tone of voice, visual language and communication strategy.

Sage

Knowledge, expertise, intelligence: helps people understand the world

Caregiver

Safety, comfort, health: takes care of others

Hero

Strength, determination: helps overcome difficulties

Creator

Innovation, uniqueness: makes something new
