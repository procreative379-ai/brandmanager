08 / Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a full brand launch cost? +
It depends on the scope. Strategy and positioning cost one amount; a full launch with packaging, certification and listings costs another. At the first meeting (it is free) we go through the task and I give you a price range. We often start with an audit: after it, it is clear what is worth doing at all.
How do you get onto the shelves of X5, Lenta and METRO? +
A buyer looks at four things: whether you can supply consistently, how much money you are ready to put into promotion, whether the price is justified (ideally with Nielsen data), and who will deal with problems on the ground. I put that package together for the specific chain and take it into the negotiations. A listing usually takes 3 to 6 months; with a package like this I have done it in 6 weeks.
Do you only work with FMCG, or with any brand? +
Most of my experience is in beverages and HoReCa equipment, so both FMCG and B2B. Positioning and a brand platform work the same way in any category, but in one I don't know I need more time to understand the market. I have worked with brands from China, Portugal and the UK.
How is a brand manager different from a marketer? +
A marketer is usually responsible for promotion and traffic. A brand manager is responsible for how the buyer sees the product and how much the brand earns. In my work that means positioning, range, pricing, listings and margin: everything that later shows up in the P&L.
Can we work remotely? +
Yes. Strategy, audits, the brand platform and preparation for retail negotiations can all be done remotely. I am based in Moscow and travel for meetings when there is no way around it.
